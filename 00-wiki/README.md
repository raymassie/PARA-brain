# Wiki

**Nothing gets filed here.** This is an index over the rest of the vault, not a place notes live.

Every note with actual content belongs in `01-projects`, `02-areas`, or `03-resources`. The wiki sits on top of those and organizes them by *topic*, which is the one view the PARA folders can't give you, because they're organized by actionability instead. A single subject like "sourdough" can have an active project, a standing area, and three resource notes scattered across three folders. The wiki note for sourdough is what pulls those into one place.

A wiki note is mostly `[[wikilinks]]`, grouped under headings, with just enough prose to say how the pieces relate. Think of a library card catalog: it tells you where things are and how they connect, and it does not hold the books.

Start new ones from `templates/wiki-note.md`, and add each one to [[Home]], the vault's start page, so it's reachable from the front door.

## What belongs in a wiki note

- Links out to notes in the live buckets, grouped by theme.
- Short connective text explaining the grouping or the relationship.
- Open questions that span several notes.

## What doesn't

- Original content that exists nowhere else. If you're writing something substantive here, it belongs in `03-resources` and the wiki note should link to it.
- Anything you'd lose by deleting this file. A wiki note should be reconstructable from the notes it points at.

Because of that last point, wiki notes are safe to correct, restructure, or delete outright. Unlike `00-raw` and `04-archive`, nothing here is a protected record.
