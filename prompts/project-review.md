# Project review

Use for “Review my project”, “What information is missing?”, or material gaps
encountered during authorized project work. Review the relevant project by
default; inspect all active projects only when asked or a linked dependency needs
it. `prompts/audit.md` handles structural and link health.

Read the canonical project note/README, goal, dates and their certainty, live task
list or designated tracker, decisions, evidence, and relevant linked notes.
Respect independent implementation repositories; inspect a specific artifact only
when needed to verify a relevant claim and access is available.

Check:

- Is the finish line concrete, and do current actions lead to it?
- Are dates firm deadlines, targets, or explicitly undecided? Do they conflict
  with known dependencies? Never infer completion from a passed date.
- Do checked tasks have suitable evidence? Distinguish user-reported progress,
  source-recorded status, directly checked output, and delivered results.
- Which blocked dependencies, stale assumptions, missing acceptance checks,
  contradictory sources, or pending decisions change the next action?
- Did completed work change the next action or a relevant linked project?
- Is there one live checklist or designated tracker, with useful history retained?

Prioritize up to three material gaps or decisions. State what is missing, why it
matters, and a concise question when an answer is needed. Reuse supplied facts;
do not ask the user to supervise routine verification or answer an explicitly
undecided date again. Continue independent authorized work while a blocking
answer is outstanding, making material reversible assumptions visible.

A review alone is read-only: return findings and questions without changing notes,
profiles, trackers, or logs. During separately authorized project work or a status
refresh, complete in-scope corrections, update the canonical checklist/status,
and follow `prompts/project-digest.md`. Preserve evidence and decisions. Stop
when the requested outcome is met or a specific blocker remains. Do not expand
the goal, commit, publish, spend, or archive merely because the review suggests it.
