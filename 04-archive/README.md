# Archive

Everything inactive: processed captures in `raw/`, and finished or abandoned
projects, areas, and resources in their matching buckets.

Existing files and folders are sealed after arrival. Never edit, overwrite, or
delete them. A newly processed capture about already finished work can become a
new uniquely named sibling note in the matching bucket, linking relevant history
without changing it. Do not add inside a sealed archived folder.

New active work stays in the appropriate live bucket even when an archived
counterpart exists. Moving existing live work into Archive follows
`prompts/archive-sweep.md` and requires confirmation. Raw captures remain unchanged
and move to `raw/` only after their ideas have been processed and checked.
